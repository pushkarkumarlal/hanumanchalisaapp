# Privacy Policy — Hanuman Chalisa

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Hanuman Chalisa is a free devotional app with no advertisements.

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

- The app has no user accounts, analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- Your jaap (mantra) count and text-size preference are saved **only on your device**
  and are never sent to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes them, unless you
  have Android's own device backup turned on (that backup is handled by Google under your
  account, not by this app).
- The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.
- The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.
- The app is not directed at children and contains no ads or in-app purchases.
- The only permission requested is **vibration**, used for gentle feedback when you tap the counter.
